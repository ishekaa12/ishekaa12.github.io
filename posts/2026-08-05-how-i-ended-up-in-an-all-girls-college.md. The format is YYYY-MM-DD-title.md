Third semester. Still don't know what a pointer is. But hey, I built a website. That counts for something, right?

## The Plot Twist

How did I even end up here?

I always wanted to go away for college, to get that whole "coming back home" feeling. The nearest parent-approved place was Bombay (yes, it's Bombae). So the obvious target was IIT Bombay — as if. I started prepping for it, spent three years behind the grind, and long story short: yes, I'm in Bombay, but not in Powai. A little further away, in Santacruz. Yes, in college — but it's an all-girls college. So my Karan Johar-infused college reality was just a mere fantasy.

## Hostel Reality Check: Single Child to 3 Roommates

Went from having my own room to sharing a fan with two people who sleep at 3 AM. Character development, fr. I was so excited to learn how to share, talk, have roomies (oh my gawd) — it was fun at the start (lol). I was the youngest in the room, so I had to listen to the other two didis. We were all so different, which meant we had a lot to talk about — each other's experiences, ideologies, hygiene, everything. I learned how to do my makeup. Or not, really — still working on it.

## The Tech Discovery Arc

College aside, I was super excited about the tech journey — the tools I'd use, the things I'd build. That's exactly what happened. C made me realize how computers actually work, and I got hooked. I figured out frontend isn't really my forte, so I explored backend tools more. Started learning DSA in Java, and it's been going from there.

## The Gut-Wrenching 2nd Sem

Second semester taught me that not everyone who smiles at you is in your corner. Learned that the hard way. I was a victim of FOMO — real about everything: the college I'd settled into, the demographic, the teaching, and something about my personal life too. Seeing others made me take a step I don't regret at all, but you only really know what's good or bad for you once you're in the situation.

The learnings were massive — too many to list — and honestly got me interested in psychology, because that much feeling with that long an impact just didn't make sense to me otherwise. But yeah, life's a trampoline of highs and lows, and boy was I bouncing. Important side note though: I turned 20. Big number, and hopefully a wiser me.

## Coming Back to Life

I'm not the same person who walked into that all-girls college in sem 1. I'm messier, more exhausted, but also... sharper? More me? Still figuring it out. Still don't know what a pointer is, if we're being honest. But at least now I'm figuring it out out loud.
